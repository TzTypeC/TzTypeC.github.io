TerraQuest

Gamified community app for sharing before-and-after eco actions

Context

A high school team project built in a 9-day sprint: students post an environmental action with before-and-after photos, and the community validates it by voting. My teammate built the UI; I built the Firebase side and the app logic.

What I built

  • Email and password authentication with registration, password-strength checks, and password reset (Firebase Auth).
  • Posting flow with two photos per post, compressed in the browser before upload to Firebase Storage.
  • Community feed from Firestore with infinite scroll and mutually exclusive upvote/downvote.

Scope note

Working prototype; the quest, guide, and guild features were planned but not implemented.
